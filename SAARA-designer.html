STARTING A PROJECT

A few useful answers.

Have an idea, a prototype or a production challenge? Here is how we can help.

Can you take a product from concept to delivery? +

Yes. We bring together product design, hardware, software, mechanics, prototyping, certification coordination, manufacturing and supply chain services. The scope is agreed around your product and business needs.

Can you help with an existing design or prototype? +

Yes. We can join at a specific stage, whether you need engineering support, preparation for manufacturing or help organising production and the supply chain.

How is manufacturing organised? +

SAARA coordinates manufacturing through our network of factories and specialist partners, supported by our offices and partners worldwide. The production setup depends on the product, quantities and required processes.

What should we send to start a conversation? +

A short description of the product, your current stage, intended markets, expected quantities and target timing is a useful starting point. You do not need a finished specification to get in touch.

Product development with SAARA

For companies developing a new device or extending an existing product, we provide the engineering needed to move forward. Start with an idea, a technical specification or a prototype. We define the next development stage with you.

01

Electronics & embedded software

We design circuit boards and the software that runs on them, from microcontroller firmware to embedded Linux. Our work includes hardware architecture, PCB design, board support packages, drivers and bootloaders.

02

Mechanical & industrial design

We develop the product enclosure, plastic parts and mechanical assemblies around the electronics and the way the device will be used. Tooling and injection moulds are coordinated with manufacturing partners.

03

Connected devices & custom subsystems

We integrate wireless connectivity, IoT gateways, custom displays and power systems. This includes custom battery packs and battery management systems designed around your device requirements.

04

Prototypes & verification

We build and test prototypes to check the design before production. Manufacturing requirements and the intended sales markets inform development, including the testing needed for certification.

Manufacturing & certification with SAARA

Have a working prototype or a design that needs to scale? We help prepare it for repeatable production, coordinating design changes, suppliers, tooling and testing with our manufacturing partners.

01

Design for manufacturing

Connect engineering decisions to component sourcing, assembly and testing. We prepare the product for repeatable manufacturing with the factories and suppliers involved in the build.

02

Tooling & custom components

Coordinate plastic injection moulds, mechanical parts, custom displays and battery assemblies. Tooling and supplier requirements are addressed alongside the product design.

03

Pilot builds & volume production

Organise prototype batches, pilot runs and series manufacturing through our production partners. Build requirements, quantities and quality checks are defined for your product.

04

Testing & certification

Coordinate product testing and the certification process, including radio compliance, with specialist partners. The required assessments depend on the product and the markets where it will be sold.

Supply chain & delivery with SAARA

For companies that need to source, manufacture and deliver a hardware product, we coordinate the suppliers and logistics partners involved. Together, we plan around your expected quantities, sales channels and delivery needs.

01

Sourcing & supplier coordination

Bring component distributors, specialist suppliers and manufacturing partners into one coordinated programme. Sourcing decisions stay connected to your engineering and production requirements.

02

Worldwide presence & partner network

Our offices and partners worldwide support coordination with local factories and suppliers. Combined with our base in Bulgaria, this connects product development with the manufacturing network.

03

Production & inventory planning

Coordinate build quantities, component availability and stockholding through our partner network. The supply arrangement is planned around your product and delivery needs.

04

Warehousing & fulfilment

Arrange storage and order fulfilment with distribution and logistics partners, from handling individual units to supporting larger shipments. The scope is agreed around your sales and distribution model.
